3. The Flaw in Generic AI Generation

WHAT THE NEW CHAT DID WRONG
  • Trapped in wireframe boxes: It framed every single sentence in heavy 10px black borders, making the design look like an unstyled HTML table.
  • Cluttered vertical spacing: Zero breathing room; all boxes had identical stroke weights.
  • Lacked editorial hierarchy: Headings and subtext had no fluid contrast or clean white space.
  • Flat geometric shapes: No continuous connector lines, no schematic diagram elegance.
THE TRUE DRAFTSRUSH AESTHETIC
  • Editorial Breathing Room: Large, spacious cards where text floats cleanly without needing borders around every single paragraph.
  • Dual-Column AV Clarity: Clean, split background blocks (Slate Blue for Visuals, Pale Butter for Dialogue) without heavy nested frames.
  • Dynamic Continuous Geometry: A single connecting timeline line with a bright milestone dot (#34D399) guiding the viewer's eye.
  • Kinetic Typography: Space Grotesk in 900 weight, balanced with micro monospaced tags (e.g. [HOOK 00:03]).
