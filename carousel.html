DAVID FINCHER

Zodiac (2007)

The Basement Encounter

WEAPONIZED POLITENESS

Bob Vaughn never threatens Graysmith. He offers hot coffee. The terror comes purely from acoustic isolation: Fincher cuts out the storm sound entirely when Graysmith hears footsteps on the floorboards above.

The Gas Station Coin Toss

ASYMMETRIC STAKES

The clerk thinks he is making awkward weather small talk. Anton Chigurh is deciding whether to execute him. Suspense explodes when only one person in the room knows life and death is on the table.
