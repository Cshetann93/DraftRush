RULE 01 // SPEAKING RATE
02 / 05

1 PAGE = 60 SECONDS.
MOST WRITERS CRAM TOO MUCH.

Standard screenplays assume 1 formatted page equals 1 minute of screen time.

In short vertical scripts, writers often pack 200+ words into 60 seconds. The speaker ends up rushing, leaving zero pauses for visual cuts.

Viewer cognitive fatigue sets in, causing retention drop-off.

Target Range
130-150 WPM

Allows natural cadence and B-roll breathing room.

Drop-Off Danger
> 175 WPM

Audio wall of text that viewers skip.

SLIDE 03 // ENTRY POINTS
RULE 02 // THE SCENE ARC
03 / 05

ARRIVE AT SECOND 10.
LEAVE AT SECOND 50.

Cut conversational throat-clearing. In a 60-second scene, you cannot afford greetings, walking up to chairs, or settling in.

Second 00:00 - 00:10 Start In Media Res

Start mid-argument or mid-action. One character is already holding the letter or slamming the desk.

Second 00:50 - 01:00 Cut On The Decision

Do not write the goodbyes or aftermath. Cut right as the character commits to the risk. Leave unresolved friction.
