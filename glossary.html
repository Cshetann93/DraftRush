Terminology Index

The definitive glossary for digital video production and scriptwriting. Learn the exact terms we use in the studio to engineer high retention videos.

← Return to the Writer's Room

A Roll

The primary video footage containing the main subject and dialogue. This forms the narrative spine of your video before any edits or cuts are applied.

AV Script

Audio Visual script. A dual column document separating spoken words on one side from visual directions on the other. This ensures editors know exactly what B Roll to use.

Order an AV Script ->

B Roll

Supplemental footage inserted over the primary audio to provide context and keep the viewer visually stimulated.

Cold Open

A scene that jumps directly into the story or conflict before the title sequence or any introductions. This is mandatory for modern YouTube retention.

Hook

The opening moments of a video designed specifically to capture attention and stop the viewer from clicking away. The strongest hooks establish the stakes in under five words.

Mini Arc

A short narrative structure within a longer video that introduces and resolves a minor point every ninety seconds. This resets viewer attention continuously.

Open Loop

Introducing a compelling question early in the script but delaying the answer. This forces viewers to keep watching to find the resolution.

See our script packages ->

Pacing

The speed at which information and visual cuts are delivered to the audience. Ideal spoken pacing sits around 130 to 160 words per minute.

Use our free pacing calculator ->

Retention Rate

The percentage of a video the average viewer watches before leaving. High retention signals to algorithms that the video is valuable, triggering broader promotion.
