Studio Background & Standards

Writing Engineered for Human Attention & Academic Rigor

DraftsRush is an independent writing and research studio. We operate at the intersection of mobile retention psychology, screenwriting discipline, and citation-verified academic analysis.

Track 01

The Scriptwriting Studio

Most online video scripts fail within the first 3 seconds due to passive scene setting and generic vocal warmups. We script directly for high hold rates across YouTube Shorts, Reels, TikTok, and long-form video essays.

Every video delivery comes formatted in dual-column AV tables with visual cutaway cues, concrete B-roll notes, and multiple hook variations tested for pacing at 130 to 160 spoken words per minute.
