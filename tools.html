Direct Benchmark Speaking Pace Formula

How many words fit into a 60-second video script?

A 60-second short-form script requires between 130 and 150 spoken words at an average conversational pace of 2.2 to 2.5 words per second. Informational documentaries pace at 130 words per minute, standard explainers pace at 150 words per minute, and high-energy social shorts allow up to 170 words per minute. Any script exceeding 180 words for a 60-second video triggers viewer fatigue and increases drop-off.

• 30-Second Reel: 65–75 words max
• 60-Second Short: 130–150 words max
• 90-Second Explainer: 200–225 words max

Direct Benchmark Screenplay Runtime Standard

How many minutes of screen time does one script page equal?

In standard film industry formatting (Courier 12pt font, 1.5-inch left margin, 1-inch right margin), one formatted screenplay page equals approximately one minute (60 seconds) of film screen time. Dialogue-heavy scenes run slightly faster at 45 to 50 seconds per page, while action-heavy or suspenseful scenes average 75 to 90 seconds per page due to non-verbal pauses and visual composition.

• 5-Page Short Film: ~5 minutes runtime
• 15-Page Festival Short: ~15–18 minutes runtime
• 110-Page Feature Script: ~1 hour 50 minutes runtime
